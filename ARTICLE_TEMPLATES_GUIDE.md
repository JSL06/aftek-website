# Article Templates Guide

## Overview
The AFTEK website now uses a template-based article system that makes it easier to create consistent, professional articles. Each template is designed for specific content types and automatically applies the appropriate styling and structure.

## Available Templates

### 📰 News Article
- **Best for**: Company updates, industry news, product launches
- **Structure**: Headline → Lead paragraph → Body content → Conclusion
- **Style**: Clean, professional, easy to scan
- **Categories**: Industry News, Product Updates, Company News

### 📝 Feature Story
- **Best for**: In-depth analysis, company stories, industry insights
- **Structure**: Engaging hook → Detailed narrative → Supporting evidence → Impact
- **Style**: Storytelling approach with rich detail
- **Categories**: Sustainability, Company News

### 💡 Technical Article
- **Best for**: Product specifications, technical guides, industry processes
- **Structure**: Problem statement → Technical solution → Implementation → Results
- **Style**: Detailed, educational, with clear explanations
- **Categories**: Technology, Technical Articles

### 📚 Case Study
- **Best for**: Success stories, project examples, customer testimonials
- **Structure**: Challenge → Solution → Implementation → Results → Lessons learned
- **Style**: Evidence-based, results-focused
- **Categories**: Case Studies

### 📊 Market Analysis
- **Best for**: Industry trends, market insights, data-driven content
- **Structure**: Market overview → Data analysis → Trends → Predictions → Recommendations
- **Style**: Analytical, data-rich, actionable insights
- **Categories**: Market Analysis

### 🌐 Opinion Piece
- **Best for**: Expert insights, industry perspectives, thought leadership
- **Structure**: Opinion statement → Supporting arguments → Examples → Conclusion
- **Style**: Persuasive, authoritative, engaging
- **Categories**: Industry News, Company News

## How to Use Templates

### 1. Choose Your Category
When creating or editing an article, select a category from the dropdown. The system automatically maps categories to appropriate templates.

### 2. Template Preview
After selecting a category, you'll see a preview of the template that will be applied, including:
- Template name and icon
- Description of the template's purpose
- Visual styling preview

### 3. Content Structure
Follow the template's recommended structure when writing your content:

#### News Article Structure:
```
Title: Clear, engaging headline
Excerpt: 2-3 sentence summary
Content: 
- Lead paragraph (who, what, when, where, why)
- Supporting details
- Quotes or expert opinions
- Conclusion with next steps
```

#### Technical Article Structure:
```
Title: Specific technical topic
Excerpt: What problem this solves
Content:
- Problem statement
- Technical background
- Solution explanation
- Implementation steps
- Results and benefits
- Technical specifications
```

#### Case Study Structure:
```
Title: Project or customer name + outcome
Excerpt: Brief success summary
Content:
- Background and challenge
- Solution approach
- Implementation process
- Results and metrics
- Key learnings
- Future applications
```

## Content Guidelines

### Title Writing
- **News**: "AFTEK Launches New Sustainable Adhesive Line"
- **Technical**: "Understanding Polyurethane Adhesives: A Complete Guide"
- **Case Study**: "How AFTEK's Adhesive Solution Saved 40% in Manufacturing Costs"

### Excerpt Writing
- Keep to 2-3 sentences
- Include the main benefit or outcome
- Use active voice
- Avoid technical jargon in non-technical articles

### Content Length
- **News**: 300-500 words
- **Feature**: 800-1200 words
- **Technical**: 1000-1500 words
- **Case Study**: 600-1000 words
- **Analysis**: 800-1200 words
- **Opinion**: 500-800 words

### Visual Elements
- Include relevant images where possible
- Use bullet points for technical specifications
- Add charts or graphs for data-heavy content
- Include product images for product-related articles

## Template Selection Tips

### Choose News Article When:
- Announcing company updates
- Sharing industry news
- Reporting on events

### Choose Feature Story When:
- Telling company stories
- Sharing industry insights
- Creating thought leadership content
- In-depth analysis of topics

### Choose Technical Article When:
- Explaining product features
- Sharing technical knowledge
- Creating educational content
- Documenting processes

### Choose Case Study When:
- Showcasing customer success
- Documenting project results
- Sharing implementation stories
- Providing proof of concept

### Choose Market Analysis When:
- Sharing industry trends
- Analyzing market data
- Making predictions
- Providing strategic insights

### Choose Opinion Piece When:
- Sharing expert opinions
- Taking industry positions
- Creating thought leadership
- Engaging in industry discussions

## Best Practices

### 1. Consistency
- Use the same template for similar content types
- Maintain consistent tone within each template
- Follow the recommended structure

### 2. Quality
- Ensure all content is accurate and up-to-date
- Include relevant keywords for SEO
- Proofread thoroughly before publishing

### 3. Engagement
- Use compelling headlines
- Include relevant images
- Make content scannable with subheadings
- End with clear calls-to-action

### 4. Multilingual Support
- Write content in all supported languages
- Ensure cultural appropriateness
- Use professional translation when needed

## Template Customization

While templates provide structure, you can customize:
- **Visual elements**: Add custom images, charts, or diagrams
- **Content length**: Adjust based on topic complexity
- **Tone**: Match your brand voice while maintaining template structure
- **Additional sections**: Add relevant subsections as needed

## Getting Started

1. **Plan your content**: Decide what type of article you're writing
2. **Choose the right template**: Select the category that best fits your content
3. **Follow the structure**: Use the template's recommended format
4. **Add your content**: Fill in all required fields with quality content
5. **Preview and publish**: Review your article and publish when ready
